## Summary

Keep the OpenFarm Connector and its command boundary. Adopt sourced catalog data, selected printer workflows and simulator scenarios. FullFarmManager has a Stage 1 Go simulator, a Next.js prototype and a useful job/run model. The snapshot does not provide real drivers or successful web integration checks. OpenFarm's baseline stays Klipper/Moonraker and Spoolman, with additional driver support graded by model, firmware and capability.

Commercial orders, products and BOM, purchasing, finance, staff and general supplies are **Cofre3D** (bloodf/cofre3d). The review covers material and consumable tracking only where it affects printer operation.

## Risks

- Protocol family names and vendor LAN workflows can hide firmware, authentication and camera differences. Record provenance and qualification per capability. Treat simulated payloads as test data.
- A successful upload or acknowledgement can precede a failed or uncertain start. Correlate with observed state and expose uncertainty without retrying. Duplicate and delayed events must not advance a plate or debit stock twice.
- Pending work can outlive authority or become unsafe when the bed, consumables or printer state changes. Admission belongs in the Connector at execution time. API commands require owner-granted scope and issuer signatures. Browser commands use WebRTC only, without TURN or HTTP fallback.
- Resin levels and usage are often unavailable. Preserve unknown values and distinguish measurements from manual entries and estimates. Never apply FDM safety or command assumptions to a resin device.
- Use original implementation and fixtures with documented provenance. Check dependency licenses and retain required notices.
