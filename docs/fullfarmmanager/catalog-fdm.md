## Differences from the prototype

- The prototype assigns Bambu MQTT to all 15 Bambu models, Creality WebSocket to all eight Creality models, and Anycubic MQTT to all four Anycubic models. These are family-level hypotheses, not model-and-firmware-specific manufacturer confirmation of protocol, ports or command authorization. Its unknown-confidence labels for newer models do not verify the others. The Snapmaker-specific family also omits the manufacturer's explicit [Klipper/Moonraker/Fluidd statement](https://wiki.snapmaker.com/en/FAQ/u1).
- K3 defaults to one tool in `profiles.ts`; Creality documents [four selectable independent nozzles](https://wiki.creality.com/en/k3-flagship-series/k3/faq). The fleet comment does describe four. X2D's two tools and H2C's two nozzles, marked unverified in the prototype, are supported by the respective manufacturer pages above. H3C's nine tools are supported by its manufacturer announcement.
- The prototype's default of one tool for other models is not independently established by this review. Accessory compatibility and slot counts, including the assumptions explicitly marked unverified in `profiles.ts`, remain unverified unless the manufacturer page cited for the individual model establishes them. Firmware versions, accessory installation, LAN-only and developer-mode states in the fleet are not measurements. The K3 and SPARKX i7 firmware comments and U1 release note were not verified here.
- The prototype's simulated telemetry payloads and support targets are illustrative, not evidence of actual printer compatibility. This review did not verify individual commands, authentication, camera transport, accessory combinations, or all model-specific network interfaces. For missing model fields the model profile index, fleet-linked maker pages and official catalog were checked; values remain unverified where those sources did not establish them.

## Driver coverage

- **Bambu MQTT:** OpenFarm has a Bambu LAN adapter with opt-in control and simulator evidence; model/firmware support and ports remain unverified against maker documentation here. See [Bambu coverage](../compatibility/bambu.md).
- **Creality stock WebSocket:** OpenFarm documents experimental read-only port 9999 telemetry for K1/K1 Max/K1C/K2/Hi, based on community sources, not manufacturer proof or hardware qualification. K3, K2 SE 4C and SPARKX i7 are not established by that coverage. See [Creality coverage](../compatibility/creality.md).
- **Anycubic MQTT:** OpenFarm documents an experimental read-only subscriber for Kobra 3/S1/4 based on community sources; it does not establish support for every fleet variant. Kobra X has a separate integration path; consult [Anycubic coverage](../compatibility/anycubic.md) and [Kobra X LAN](../compatibility/kobra-x-lan.md).
- **Moonraker:** U1 runs manufacturer-customized Klipper/Moonraker/Fluidd; OpenFarm has generic Moonraker support and reports read-only U1 telemetry qualification, not blanket command or firmware support. Rooted K1 is a separate configuration, not an assertion about stock K1C 2025. See [Moonraker coverage](../compatibility/klipper.md). Port 7125 is not verified for the U1 by the cited manufacturer FAQ.
